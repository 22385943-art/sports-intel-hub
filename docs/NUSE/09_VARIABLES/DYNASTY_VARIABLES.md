# Dynasty Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every dynasty-related variable recognized by the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Within NUSE, a dynasty represents a sustained period of organizational excellence achieved through long-term competitive success, roster continuity, player development and strategic decision-making.

Dynasties emerge from cumulative organizational processes rather than isolated championship seasons.

Dynasties are long-term phenomena.

# Final Statement

Dynasty variables represent the long-term organizational success of NBA franchises within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine.

Rather than defining dynasties solely through championship counts, NUSE models them as emergent organizational systems sustained by competitive consistency, financial discipline, talent renewal, leadership continuity and strategic stability. This framework enables realistic simulation of franchise legacies, competitive eras and long-term organizational evolution while preserving explainability and causal consistency throughout the engine.
