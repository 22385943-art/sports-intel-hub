# Player Synergy Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every variable describing synergy between players within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Player synergy represents the emergent interaction between two or more players sharing possessions.

Unlike player ability, synergy is relational.

A player's effectiveness may increase or decrease depending on the teammates surrounding them.

Within NUSE, synergy influences offensive execution, defensive coordination and lineup performance.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Synergy is relational.

Synergy is emergent.

Synergy evolves over time.

Synergy depends on lineup context.

Synergy SHALL be independent from individual player ratings.

Synergy SHALL remain probabilistic.

# 12. General Rules

Player synergy variables SHALL:

Represent relationships between players.

Remain independent from player talent.

Support deterministic replay.

Support probabilistic simulation.

Support Bayesian updating.

Evolve through shared experience.

Influence lineup performance.

Remain explainable.

---

# Final Statement

Player synergy variables define the emergent relationships between teammates within NUSE.

Rather than assuming that players contribute independently, NUSE models synergy as a dynamic relational process generated by shared possessions, tactical compatibility, communication and mutual adaptation. This allows lineup performance to emerge naturally from interactions between players while preserving causal consistency throughout the simulation engine.
