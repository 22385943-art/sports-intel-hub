# Team Traditional Statistics

## Purpose

This document defines every traditional team statistic used by the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Unlike player statistics, team statistics describe the collective output generated by all players, coaching decisions and game context.

These variables form the foundation for team projections and season simulations.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Team statistics are emergent properties.

They result from:

- Player talent
- Rotation decisions
- Coaching system
- Opponent quality
- Pace
- Health
- Chemistry
- Schedule

The same roster can produce different team statistics under different contexts.

# 18. General Rules

Traditional team variables SHALL:

Represent observed production.

Remain independent from advanced calculations.

Support per-game, per-possession and per-100 normalization.

Preserve temporal ordering.

Remain comparable across seasons.

---

# Final Statement

Traditional team statistics form the first descriptive layer of collective team performance.

Within NUSE they provide the observable outputs that later interact with advanced, lineup, coaching and contextual models to generate complete team projections.
