# Front Office Staff Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every front office staff-related variable recognized by the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Within NUSE, the Front Office Staff represents the organizational department supporting executive basketball decision-making through administration, salary cap management, legal expertise, contract execution, financial planning and organizational coordination.

Front Office Staff variables describe the collective executive support capability of an NBA franchise.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Front office staff support executive decision-making.

Operational excellence improves organizational consistency.

Institutional knowledge accumulates over time.

Front office quality influences every long-term organizational decision.

Front Office variables SHALL represent organizational capability.

Front Office variables SHALL remain explainable.

# Final Statement

Front Office Staff variables represent the collective executive infrastructure of NBA organizations within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine.

Rather than attributing organizational success solely to the General Manager, NUSE models the Front Office Staff as an evolving organizational system whose expertise in salary cap management, contract administration, legal compliance, financial planning and executive support enables consistent, explainable and sustainable franchise decision-making throughout the simulation engine.
