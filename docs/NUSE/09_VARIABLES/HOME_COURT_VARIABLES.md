# Home Court Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every variable describing home-court advantage within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Within NUSE, home-court advantage is modeled as an emergent latent phenomenon.

It does not originate from a single source.

Instead, it emerges from the interaction of arena familiarity, crowd behavior, travel asymmetry, environmental adaptation, officiating tendencies and psychological comfort.

Home-court advantage modifies probabilities rather than determining outcomes.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Home-court advantage is emergent.

Home-court advantage varies between teams.

Home-court advantage varies over time.

Home-court advantage is context dependent.

Home-court advantage SHALL never guarantee victory.

Home-court advantage SHALL be explainable through measurable variables.

# Final Statement

Home-court variables define the emergent competitive advantage associated with playing at home within NUSE.

Rather than treating home-court advantage as a fixed bonus, NUSE models it as a latent probabilistic process generated by the interaction of arena familiarity, crowd dynamics, travel asymmetry, psychological comfort and tactical continuity. This approach enables realistic and explainable simulation of home performance while preserving causal consistency throughout the engine.
