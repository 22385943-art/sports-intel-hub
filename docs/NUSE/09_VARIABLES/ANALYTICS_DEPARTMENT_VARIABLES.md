# Analytics Department Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every analytics department-related variable recognized by the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Within NUSE, the Analytics Department represents the organizational system responsible for transforming basketball data into actionable knowledge that supports coaching, scouting, roster construction and long-term organizational decision-making.

Analytics variables describe the collective analytical capability of a franchise rather than individual statistical models.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Analytics is decision support.

Analytics complements human expertise.

Analytics evolves continuously.

Analytics quality depends on data quality.

Analytics variables SHALL represent organizational capability.

Analytics variables SHALL remain explainable.

# Final Statement

Analytics department variables represent the collective analytical intelligence of NBA organizations within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine.

Rather than treating analytics as isolated statistical reports, NUSE models analytics departments as evolving organizational systems that integrate data infrastructure, predictive modeling, research and decision support into every level of basketball operations. This framework enables realistic organizational intelligence, continuous learning and explainable decision-making while preserving causal consistency throughout the simulation engine.
