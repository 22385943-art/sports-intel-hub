# Possession Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every variable describing an individual possession within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

A possession is the atomic simulation unit of basketball.

Every game is composed of possessions.

Every season is ultimately composed of millions of possessions.

Every player projection, team projection and simulation generated by NUSE is an aggregation of possession-level outcomes.

---

# 1. Core Principles

A possession begins when one team gains offensive control of the ball.

A possession ends when:

- A made basket is scored.
- A defensive rebound is secured.
- A turnover occurs.
- The period ends.
- Free throws conclude possession according to NBA rules.

Every possession is independent in execution but dependent on context.

# 21. General Rules

Possession variables SHALL:

Represent exactly one possession.

Remain temporally ordered.

Support deterministic replay.

Support probabilistic simulation.

Remain reproducible.

Allow uncertainty propagation.

Support historical reconstruction.

---

# Final Statement

Possession variables define the smallest complete unit of basketball recognized by NUSE.

Every game simulation, player projection, team projection and season outcome ultimately emerges from the interaction and aggregation of millions of modeled possessions, making this document one of the foundational specifications of the entire simulation engine.
