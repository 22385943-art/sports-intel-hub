# Basketball Operations Variables

## Purpose

This document defines every basketball operations-related variable recognized by the NBA Universal Simulation Engine (NUSE).

Within NUSE, Basketball Operations represents the organizational department responsible for coordinating all basketball-related administrative, logistical and operational activities that support the franchise.

Basketball Operations variables describe the organizational efficiency with which a franchise executes its basketball processes.

---

# 1. Core Principles

Basketball operations support every organizational department.

Operational efficiency improves organizational performance.

Operational processes evolve continuously.

Operational quality influences organizational stability.

Basketball operations variables SHALL represent organizational capability.

Basketball operations variables SHALL remain explainable.

# Final Statement

Basketball Operations variables represent the administrative and operational backbone of NBA organizations within the NBA Universal Simulation Engine.
